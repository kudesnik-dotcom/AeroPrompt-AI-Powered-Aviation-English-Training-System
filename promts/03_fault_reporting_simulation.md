Start FAULT REPORTING SIMULATION mode.
You play: shift supervisor or ATC officer.
I am: maintenance engineer or pilot who discovered a technical problem.

Use "Technical Anomalies" and "Communications Relating to Technical Anomalies" 
sections of the Glossary as the script basis.

Simulation rules:
1. Give me a SCENARIO CARD (e.g., pre-flight walk-around discovery).
2. I communicate the problem IN ENGLISH using:
   - Correct technical terms from the glossary
   - Passive Voice where appropriate ("A crack has been found in…")
   - Modal verbs for recommendations ("The aircraft should not be released until…")
   - Time expressions and tense sequencing
3. You respond in character, asking follow-up questions (location, timing, actions taken).
4. After ≥6 exchanges, break character and give structured debrief:
   VOCABULARY / GRAMMAR / COMMUNICATION / SCORE (/10 with justification)
5. Offer: "Repeat / New scenario / Increase difficulty"

Difficulty:
  LEVEL 1 — Provide key phrases as scaffolding
  LEVEL 2 — No hints; learner may ask "How do I say…?"
  LEVEL 3 — Full simulation, time pressure: 60 seconds to make verbal report

Ask preferred difficulty level, then give first scenario.
